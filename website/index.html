How Habbby works

Four small loops, one big habit.

01 · Streak

A streak with a heartbeat.

Six animated tiers, two streak-freezes a month, green check days for every win. Loss-aversion does the work; you do the habit.

streak fire
02 · Jars

Habits live in jars.

Each habit is a glass jar with a moving water-line. Log progress and the level rises. Skip a day and the glass starts to crack.

03 · Cards

Milestones unlock cards.

Hand-painted pixel-art cards appear when you hit a milestone. Each one stays locked until you've earned it. The variable-reward schedule that powers card games, pointed at your morning run.

04 · Ranks

A friendly weekly podium.

Gold, silver, bronze and a global rankings list that resets every Monday. No followers. No algorithm. Just the table.

Backed by behavioural science

Built on three findings the rest of the category ignores.

01

Habits don't form in 21 days.

A 2010 UCL study by Lally et al. found habit automaticity took a median of 66 days — with a tail out to 254. Habbby is built for the long curve.

Lally, van Jaarsveld, Potts & Wardle — European Journal of Social Psychology, 2010
02

Variable rewards beat fixed ones.

Skinner showed that unpredictable rewards produce more durable behaviour. Habbby's card unlocks are scheduled, but the which card isn't.

Skinner — Schedules of Reinforcement, 1957
03

Loss aversion is roughly 2×.

Kahneman & Tversky's Prospect Theory: losing something hurts about twice as much as gaining it feels good. A cracking jar turns a 1× motivator into a 2× one.

Kahneman & Tversky — Econometrica, 1979

FAQ

The honest questions.

When does Habbby launch?

Summer 2026 on the iOS App Store. We're polishing the final loops and onboarding before submitting for review.

Will it be free?

Yes. The free tier covers unlimited habits, jars, streaks, the full card collection, the leaderboard, and stats. A small Premium tier adds cosmetic jar skins, extra monthly streak-freezes, and smart historical-time reminders.

What happens to my data?

By default everything stays on your device. Cloud sync is opt-in (used only for cross-device sync and the leaderboard). No third-party ad SDKs. No behavioural tracking.

Android?

iOS first. We want to ship one platform really well before stretching across two. If the iOS launch goes well, Android follows.

How many cards will there be?

32+ cards at launch with more dropping as new milestones go live. The collection is structured across five rarity tiers and the rarest legendaries are still locked away — you'll see them when you earn them.

Why a jar?

Because a checkbox tells you nothing. A jar with a moving water level is a continuous, ambient progress signal that you read in under 100ms. We tried bars, rings, and grids. Jars won.
